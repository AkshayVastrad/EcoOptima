EcoOptima: IoT energy management for German SMEs

Turning energy waste into green business value.

EcoOptima is a concept under development. Savings figures on this page are market estimates, not measured results.

Message me on LinkedIn

The problem

  • Energy is wasted. Old equipment and a lack of data can cost small businesses an estimated 15–30% of their energy.
  • Manual audits are slow and expensive. Many SMEs never get a detailed picture of where energy goes.
  • Regulation adds pressure. Energy efficiency rules (EED) and sustainability reporting (CSRD) create compliance work that SMEs often meet through their customers.

The solution

EcoOptima is a plug-and-play system that closes the loop between measuring and acting on energy use.

  • MonitorIoT sensors collect real-time energy data.
  • AnalyzeAn AI cloud platform analyzes, predicts and finds waste.
  • ActAutonomous control adjusts HVAC, lighting and devices.

Standard smart meters collect and visualize consumption, then leave the interpretation to you. EcoOptima is designed to go one step further.

Planned architecture

Four layers, with data flowing up and control commands flowing down. Control keeps running locally if the internet connection drops.

Business model

Two subscription tiers, plus an optional carbon service.

Lite

Monitoring and alerts for a fixed monthly fee, so costs stay predictable and risk stays low.

Pro

Full optimization with AI control, priced as a share of the energy savings and bundled with carbon brokerage.

Carbon brokerage. The platform is meant to verify a client’s energy and carbon savings, aggregate them into carbon credits and sell them on the client’s behalf. Whether savings qualify as credits depends on recognized verification standards, so this part comes after verified savings reports.

Market. Germany has about 3.5 million SMEs. The 15–30% savings potential is an estimate to be tested in pilots.

Roadmap

Planned, not yet delivered.

  1. Validate the problem with SME energy data and interviews.
  2. Build the monitoring tier (Lite): meters, gateway, dashboard and alerts.
  3. Add baseline forecasting and anomaly detection.
  4. Add controlled optimization (Pro), starting with advice before autonomous control.
  5. Add verified savings reports and carbon accounting.
  6. Run pilots with SME customers.
